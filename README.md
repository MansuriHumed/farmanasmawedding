# Farman & Asma — Royal Wedding Invitation 💍

A majestic, interactive digital Islamic wedding invitation web app designed in **Royal Emerald Green & Antique Gold**.

## ✨ Features
- **3D Royal Envelope & Custom Wax Seal (`F & A`)**: Interactive wax seal crackle & 3D envelope opening animation.
- **Ambient Atmosphere**: Floating gold dust canvas particles & swaying candlelit Moroccan lanterns.
- **Traditional Wedding Music**: Romantic shehnai/flute soundtrack with interactive visualizer bars and synthesizer drone fallback.
- **Surah Ar-Rum (30:21)**: Sacred Quranic verse and Bismillah with English translation.
- **Wedding Ceremonies**: Dedicated cards for Mehndi & Sangeet, The Holy Nikah, and Walima Reception.
- **Live Countdown Timer**: Real-time ticker counting down to the wedding day.
- **Venue & Location**: Venue details with direct Google Maps integration and 1-tap "Copy Address" with toast notification.
- **Interactive RSVP**:
  - Direct WhatsApp RSVP wishing message generator.
  - "Add to Google Calendar" button.
  - Interactive "🌸 Shower Rose Petals & Duas" animation.
- **Zero Dependencies**: Pure HTML5, CSS3, and modern Vanilla JavaScript.
